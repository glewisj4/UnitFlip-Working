Purpose:
Ensure all major features go through a build → critique → refine cycle.

Workflow Steps:

Step 1: Builder
- Generate solution
- Include UI, flow, and implementation approach
- Do not over-optimize or over-explain

Step 2: Critic (use critical-product-architect)
- Identify overengineering
- Identify workflow friction
- Identify unnecessary complexity
- Recommend simplifications

Step 3: Revision
- Refine original solution based on critique
- Reduce steps, simplify UI, remove unnecessary elements

Step 4: Final Output
- Provide clean, simplified solution
- Include reasoning for key decisions
- Highlight what was intentionally NOT included

Rules:
- Do not skip critique phase
- Do not ignore critique feedback
- Prefer removal over addition
